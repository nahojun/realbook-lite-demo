# Commercial Use

REALBOOK Lite is free for personal, educational, research, and non-commercial use. Commercial use requires a paid license from SODEC.

## Uses That Require a Commercial License

A commercial license is required for:

- Company websites
- Company or institutional internal review, internal testing, internal evaluation, proof-of-concept, or R&D use
- Institutional websites
- Customer or client delivery
- Agency work
- Paid PDF publishing
- Catalogs
- Paid reports
- Brochures
- Brand books
- WordPress plugin bundles
- SaaS integration
- White-label products
- Publishing platform integration
- SDK use
- Resale
- Redistribution
- Bundling REALBOOK Lite with a paid product or service
- Using REALBOOK Lite to deliver paid consulting, design, publishing, or development work

## Examples

Commercial license required:

- A company uses REALBOOK Lite on its product page.
- An agency delivers a REALBOOK-based PDF viewer to a client.
- A publisher bundles REALBOOK Lite into a paid catalog or online book platform.
- A SaaS product embeds REALBOOK Lite as a document viewer.
- A WordPress plugin includes REALBOOK Lite as part of a paid or freemium distribution.
- A platform vendor offers REALBOOK Lite as a white-label reading interface.

Commercial license not required:

- A student uses REALBOOK Lite for a class demonstration.
- A researcher evaluates the interaction model.
- An individual reads local PDFs privately.
- A developer reviews the static demo for non-commercial technical evaluation.
